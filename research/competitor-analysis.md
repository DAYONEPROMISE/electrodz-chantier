# Competitor Analysis

| Product | Strengths | Weaknesses vs ElectroDZ | Pricing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ElectroCalc (arabvolt) | 15 langs, offline, cable sizing + VD | Not DZ-specific; reports/PDF paywalled direction; no intervention fiches | Freemium |
| Ampero (zukasoft) | IEC 60364, 11 modules | iOS-first (Android "in development"); Pro paywall; English-first | Freemium + Pro |
| Voltix (getvoltix.app) | 12 calculators, offline Android | NEC/BS/AS-NZS standards, not IEC/C15-100 metric-first; subscription | Free core + Pro sub |
| Voltage Drop Calc NEC | Accurate NEC tables | NEC/AWG only, useless for DZ mm² practice; paid | SAR 4.99 + IAP |
| MouqawilPro / Dolisoft / Dinateck / Almawarid / Rochemère (DZ) | DZ billing, DZD, FR/AR, TVA/CNAS | SaaS billing/ERP, subscriptions, need internet; NO field cable-sizing tool | ~4 500 DA/mo or licence |

Differentiation: 100% offline, French + Arabic RTL, IEC metric (220/380V, mm² Cu/Al),
no account, no subscription, calculator + chantier paperwork (clients, intervention
fiches, history, PDF export) in one field tool. What we do NOT do: invoicing, TVA,
CNAS, cloud sync (saturated + regulated).
